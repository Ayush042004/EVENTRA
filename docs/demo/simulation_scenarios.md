# Simulation Scenarios

Authentic simulation scenarios test the entire EVENTRA pipeline by injecting realistic incidents:

1. **Vendor No-Show**: Critical AV or catering vendor fails to arrive. System checks dependencies, elevates severity to EMERGENCY, identifies backup vendor within budget cap, generates approval ticket, and verifies on-site arrival.
2. **Venue Issue**: Severe weather or physical room issue forces space change. System evaluates alternative venues, checks capacity and travel ETA, recalculates setup times, routes approval, and notifies stakeholders.
3. **Resource Shortage**: Delivered supplies are damaged or short. System calculates delta against required capacity, finds local rental alternatives, evaluates autonomous spending limit, dispatches courier order, and verifies receipt.
